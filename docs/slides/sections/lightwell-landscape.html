The Lightwell Story

Why Lightwell Exists

Frontier AI collapsed Time-To-Exploit to near zero. When AI models can autonomously discover zero-day vulnerabilities and weave working exploits faster than any human research team, manual patch testing becomes a relic. Organizations need defenses that operate at the same speed as the threat.

Three Options, All Insufficient

▲ Upgrade

Jump to the latest major version where the fix already exists upstream.

Risk: Breaking API changes cascade through production. The fix must already exist upstream — and it often doesn’t.

⚒ DIY Backport

Cherry-pick and manually adapt the patch to your pinned version.

Risk: You now own a custom fork forever. Maintenance cost compounds with every subsequent CVE.

⚠ Do Nothing

Accept the vulnerability and rely on compensating controls.

Risk: Confirmed exploits remain in production. Regulatory exposure and audit findings accumulate.

Four Operating Principles

✅ Every Fix Goes Upstream

Lightwell contributes every patch back to the open-source project. No permanent forks, no walled gardens.

⏳ Short Embargo Windows

14-day default embargo. Patches reach the community quickly while giving subscribers a head start.

🔒 Strict Confidentiality & Anonymization

Vulnerability submissions are anonymized. No organization’s identity or inventory is ever shared.

🤝 Community Partnership

Lightwell works with upstream maintainers, not around them. Fixes follow project contribution guidelines.

Key takeaway: Lightwell doesn’t replace your scanner — it takes the findings and provides the non-breaking patches your developers need to resolve them.
